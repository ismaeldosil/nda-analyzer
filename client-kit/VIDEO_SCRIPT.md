# NDA Analyzer - Video Script

> Talking points for the demo video. Follow this script when recording.

---

## Video Overview

**Duration**: ~5-7 minutes
**Audience**: PE firms evaluating the NDA Analyzer
**Goal**: Show how easy it is to use, explain the output, mention assets are available

---

## INTRO (30 seconds)

### What to Say:
> "Hi, I'm going to show you how to use the M&A NDA Analyzer. This tool helps PE firms quickly identify risks in NDAs before signing.
>
> By the end of this video, you'll see how to get instant analysis of any NDA. And I'll share all the resources you need to use this yourself."

### Key Points:
- Tool is for PE firms doing M&A
- Identifies risks quickly
- Resources will be provided

---

## SECTION 1: Accessing the Tool (1 minute)

### What to Show:
- Open the Gemini Gem link
- Show the interface

### What to Say:
> "First, let's access the analyzer. I'm using Google Gemini - it's free, you just need a Google account.
>
> I'll share this link with you so you can access it directly. Just click the link, sign in with Google, and you're ready to go.
>
> **Note**: All the links, prompts, and sample files I'm using today will be provided to you separately, so you can set this up yourself or use our hosted version."

### Key Points:
- It's free (Google account required)
- Link will be shared
- All assets will be provided

---

## SECTION 2: Uploading an NDA (1 minute)

### What to Show:
- Paste or upload the sample NDA (Project Atlas)
- Type "Analyze this NDA"

### What to Say:
> "Now I'll paste an NDA. This is a sample M&A NDA - I'll provide this sample file too so you can test it yourself.
>
> I just type 'Analyze this NDA' and hit enter. The analyzer will review the document and generate a risk matrix.
>
> The default settings are: Buyer perspective, which is what PE firms typically want. It's optimized for Delaware law, which is the standard for M&A transactions."

### Key Points:
- Can paste or upload NDA
- Sample NDA will be provided
- Default: Buyer perspective, Delaware-optimized

---

## SECTION 3: Understanding the Output (2-3 minutes)

### What to Show:
- The Risk Matrix output
- Walk through each section

### What to Say:

#### 3.1 Overall Assessment
> "First, look at the top. It shows the document name and tells us the Recommended Action is 'Negotiate'. This means there are issues we should address before signing."

#### 3.2 Must Address Section
> "This 'Must Address' section lists the critical items. Let me walk through them:
>
> - **Term: 3 years** - Standard is 2 years or less. Three years extends our compliance burden unnecessarily.
> - **Governing Law: New York** - We prefer Delaware because of their specialized Chancery Court that handles M&A disputes efficiently.
> - **Non-Circumvention clause present** - This is a red flag. There's case law - Goodrich Capital v. Vector Capital - where a non-circumvention clause created $3.5 million in unexpected liability.
> - **PE Acknowledgement missing** - As a PE firm, we need this clause to protect our ability to invest in competitive companies through other portfolio investments."

#### 3.3 The Risk Matrix Table
> "Below that is the detailed matrix. Each clause is marked:
> - **OK** means it's acceptable
> - **FLAG** with the exclamation mark means it needs attention
>
> Notice Non-Solicitation is marked OK - that's because this NDA has the right exceptions for general advertising and search firms, which is what we want."

#### 3.4 Suggested Additions
> "At the bottom, the analyzer automatically generates suggested text for missing clauses. Here's the PE Acknowledgement language we should request. This saves time - instead of drafting from scratch, we have ready-to-use language."

### Key Points:
- Explain each FLAG and why it matters
- Reference case law (Goodrich Capital)
- Show OK items too (balanced analysis)
- Highlight auto-generated text

## Quick Reference: Talking Points for Output Analysis

When showing the sample NDA results, explain these findings:

| Finding | What to Say |
|---------|-------------|
| **Governing Law: NY** | "We prefer Delaware - specialized courts, predictable M&A precedent" |
| **Term: 3 years** | "Market standard is 2 years or less - this extends our compliance burden" |
| **Non-Circumvention** | "Red flag. Goodrich Capital case - $3.5M unexpected fee. We want this removed" |
| **Return of CI** | "No retention allowed - problematic for compliance. We need to keep copies for legal/regulatory purposes" |
| **PE Ack missing** | "Critical for PE firms - protects our ability to invest in competitive businesses" |
| **Non-Solicit OK** | "This one's fine - has the right carve-outs for general ads and recruiters" |

## Recording Tips

1. **Pace**: Speak slowly, pause between sections
2. **Screen**: Make text large enough to read
3. **Cursor**: Point to what you're discussing
4. **Tone**: Conversational, not salesy
5. **Length**: Aim for 5-7 minutes total
