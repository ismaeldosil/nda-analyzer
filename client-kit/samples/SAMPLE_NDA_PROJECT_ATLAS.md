# CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

## Project Atlas

**This Confidentiality Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

**Atlas Manufacturing Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 500 Industrial Boulevard, Chicago, Illinois 60601 (the "Company" or "Discloser")

and

**[Prospective Buyer]**, a Delaware limited partnership (the "Recipient" or "Potential Acquirer")

(each a "Party" and collectively, the "Parties")

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company is exploring strategic alternatives, which may include a potential sale, merger, or other business combination transaction (the "Transaction"); and

WHEREAS, in connection with the Recipient's evaluation of a possible Transaction, the Company is prepared to make available to Recipient certain confidential and proprietary information concerning the Company;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any and all information, whether written, oral, electronic, visual, or in any other form, that is disclosed by or on behalf of the Company to the Recipient or its Representatives in connection with the evaluation of the Transaction, including but not limited to:

(a) financial statements, projections, budgets, and forecasts;
(b) business plans, strategies, and operational information;
(c) customer and supplier lists, contracts, and relationships;
(d) proprietary technology, trade secrets, patents, and intellectual property;
(e) employee information and organizational data;
(f) any analyses, compilations, studies, or other documents prepared by Recipient or its Representatives that contain or reflect such information.

### 1.2 Representatives

"Representatives" means the directors, officers, employees, agents, advisors (including attorneys, accountants, consultants, and financial advisors), and controlling persons of a Party.

### 1.3 Affiliate

"Affiliate" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person. For purposes of this definition, "control" means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct or cause the direction of the management and policies of a Person, whether through the ownership of voting securities, by contract, or otherwise.

---

## 2. USE OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

The Recipient agrees that it shall use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating a possible transaction between the Parties and for no other purpose whatsoever. The Recipient shall not use any Confidential Information in any way that is detrimental to the Company or for any competitive purpose.

---

## 3. NON-DISCLOSURE OBLIGATIONS

### 3.1 Confidentiality

The Recipient agrees to keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any Person, except to those of its Representatives who (a) need to know such information for the purpose of evaluating the Transaction, and (b) are informed of the confidential nature of such information and agree to be bound by the terms of this Agreement.

### 3.2 Standard of Care

The Recipient shall protect the Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

### 3.3 Responsibility for Representatives

The Recipient shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by any of its Representatives. Any action or inaction by a Representative that would constitute a breach of this Agreement if taken by the Recipient shall be deemed a breach by the Recipient.

---

## 4. NON-SOLICITATION OF EMPLOYEES

### 4.1 Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of eighteen (18) months thereafter, the Recipient shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit, hire, or attempt to hire any employee of the Company with whom the Recipient has had contact or about whom the Recipient has received Confidential Information.

### 4.2 Exceptions

The restrictions in Section 4.1 shall not apply to:
(a) general solicitations of employment through advertisements in newspapers, trade publications, or online job boards that are not specifically directed at Company employees;
(b) solicitations conducted by recruiting or search firms that are not specifically directed at Company employees; or
(c) any employee who contacts the Recipient on his or her own initiative without any direct or indirect solicitation by the Recipient.

---

## 5. NON-CIRCUMVENTION

The Recipient agrees that it shall not, directly or indirectly, contact, deal with, or otherwise circumvent the Company in any manner with respect to any customers, suppliers, business partners, or other third parties introduced to the Recipient by the Company or identified through the Confidential Information. The Recipient acknowledges that the Company has invested substantial time and resources in developing these relationships, and any attempt to circumvent the Company would cause irreparable harm. This non-circumvention obligation shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

---

## 6. RETURN OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

### 6.1 Return or Destruction

Upon the written request of the Company, or upon termination of discussions regarding the Transaction, the Recipient shall promptly:
(a) return to the Company all documents, materials, and other tangible items containing or reflecting Confidential Information;
(b) destroy all copies, extracts, and summaries of Confidential Information in the Recipient's possession or control; and
(c) provide written certification of such return and destruction signed by an authorized officer of the Recipient.

### 6.2 Complete Destruction Required

For the avoidance of doubt, the Recipient shall not retain any copies of Confidential Information for any purpose, including for archival, compliance, or backup purposes. All electronic copies must be permanently deleted from all systems, servers, and storage devices.

---

## 7. NO REPRESENTATION OR WARRANTY

The Company makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of the Confidential Information. The Recipient agrees that neither the Company nor any of its Representatives shall have any liability to the Recipient or its Representatives relating to or arising from the use of or reliance upon any Confidential Information.

---

## 8. REMEDIES

The Recipient acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to the Company for which monetary damages would be inadequate. Accordingly, the Company shall be entitled to seek equitable relief, including injunction and specific performance, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

---

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 9.1 Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Section 9.2.

### 9.2 Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing written notice to the other Party. Upon termination, the obligations of confidentiality and non-disclosure shall survive for a period of three (3) years from the date of termination.

---

### 10.2 Jurisdiction

### 10.3 Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the Parties.

### 10.4 Amendment

This Agreement may not be amended or modified except by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

### 10.5 Waiver

No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right, power, or privilege under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver thereof.

### 10.6 Assignment

This Agreement may not be assigned by either Party without the prior written consent of the other Party.

### 10.7 Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
