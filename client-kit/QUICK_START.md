# NDA Analyzer - Quick Start

> Get instant M&A NDA risk analysis in 30 seconds

---

## Use It Now

**Step 1**: Click this link:
> **https://gemini.google.com/gem/1eZK-8lANbx0-i_cvSbSbVyfsNyVj3n8u?usp=sharing**

**Step 2**: Sign in with your Google account (free)

**Step 3**: Paste your NDA and type: `Analyze this NDA`

**Step 4**: Get your Risk Matrix

---


---

## What It Checks

| Clause | What We Want |
|--------|--------------|
| Governing Law | Delaware |
| Term | 2 years or less |
| Non-Solicitation | Has exceptions for ads/agencies |
| Non-Circumvention | NOT present |
| Return of Info | Allows retention for compliance |
| PE Acknowledgement | Present |

## Quick Commands

| Type This | To Get |
|-----------|--------|
| `Analyze this NDA` | Full risk analysis |
| `Analyze from Seller perspective` | Seller-focused analysis |
| `OUTPUT=full` | Detailed findings |
| `Generate PE Acknowledgement for [Company]` | Ready-to-use clause |
